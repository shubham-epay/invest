# Invest.to — Brand guidelines

Version 3.3 · 12 October 2026

## Brand foundation

Brand: **Invest.to** (wordmark: **invest.to**).

Brand line: **Global markets. Your perspective.**

Positioning: A forex and CFD trading platform with a clear, considered and approachable experience.

Operating entity supplied by the owner: **Eptra Prime Ltd**, Saint Lucia. The owner-supplied registration statement is: Invest.to (Eptra Prime Ltd) is registered under Saint Lucia Registry of International Business Companies. Registration number, registered address and regulatory permissions have not been independently verified in this brand guide.

The homepage uses Capital.com’s UAE website as a reference for large-scale typography, immersive sections and layered interface motion, with Invest.to’s own red, charcoal, navy and white colour system and Poppins typography. The earlier CMS Prime reference informed the clean brokerage typography; SynraPay informed the red accent. It has its own wordmark, mark, artwork and copy. It does not imply a corporate relationship with any reference brand.

## Logo

The Perspective identity is a flat red mark paired with an outlined Poppins Medium wordmark. Two offset corners frame an open space, expressing focus and an individual point of view. The red `.to` identifies the digital destination.

- Primary: ink navy lettering with red mark and `.to`, for white and light surfaces.
- Reversed: white lettering with red mark and `.to`, for dark surfaces.
- Monochrome: single-colour dark and white variants for restricted production.
- Use SVG on the website and transparent PNG in documents or presentations. Lettering is outlined and does not require an installed font.
- App icon: the matching Perspective mark is supplied as SVG and PNG at 32, 192, 512 and 1024 px.
- Preserve proportions. Do not add gradients, bevels, shadows, rotation or extrusion.
- Leave clear space of at least half the mark's height around the lockup.
- Suggested minimum full-logo width: 120 px. Use the mark alone below this size.
- The previous stepped-bar and 3D identities are superseded; do not combine them with this identity.

Lead with white and charcoal. Use red sparingly. Use action red for normal-size white button text; the brighter signal red is primarily a brand accent. Gold imagery uses natural metallic textures and a red accent. Supporting illustrations use matte red, charcoal linework and a light paper ground. The logo always remains flat. Avoid glossy orbs, chrome spheres and generic floating objects.

## Typography

Poppins throughout. Actual font files and the SIL Open Font License are included.

- Headlines: Poppins Medium 500 or SemiBold 600. The brokerage hero uses responsive Poppins Medium lettering with clear space around the headline.
- Body: Poppins Regular 400, generally 16–18 px. Preserve comfortable line spacing and contrast.
- Controls: Medium 500, generally 14 px or larger.
- Secondary metadata: 12 px minimum where practical.
- Brokerage hero: responsive 46–72 px on desktop and 42–64 px on mobile, with a line height of 1.14. Text may wrap naturally on narrow screens.
- Section headings: 35–49 px depending on viewport.
- Avoid excessive capitals; use uppercase only for short section labels.

## Layout and components

Use a compact charcoal header, a dark split hero, centred section introductions, generous margins, fine borders and a clear page hierarchy. Alternate white and paper sections; retain the deep navy Market Watch. Navigation opens in a drawer, with site search available from the header. A maximum web content width of 1280 px keeps long lines manageable. Collapse multi-column sections cleanly on small screens.

Buttons have a modest 7 px corner radius. Cards use 10–14 px corners. Keep one dominant action within a group. Use text links or neutral outlined buttons for supporting actions. Provide visible keyboard focus and reduce motion when requested by the device.

## Artwork

The homepage hero uses original layered interface concepts in perspective. The world section includes a real WebGL globe with Natural Earth land geometry, and the platform section combines illustrative desktop and mobile workspaces. All interface charts are clearly labelled as illustrations. Motion responds to the pointer and scroll, with automatic reduced-motion support. The flat logo must never be rotated, extruded or altered; depth belongs to the interface surfaces. The earlier fictional gold-trading workstation artwork remains available as supporting imagery. An anonymous financial-district image supports the dark hero background and closing section. Neither image establishes an office location, real client, working platform or trading result. Avoid generic orbs and floating abstract objects. Do not overlay fictional chart data on photography. The platform section includes a separate labelled interface concept with an illustrative chart and no quote or profit values.

The Market Desk uses TradingView Advanced Chart with OANDA EUR/USD, XAU/USD and XAG/USD data. Give the chart the full content width, with compact instrument selectors above it. The surrounding section is deep navy, the chart white, and the active selector red. Preserve provider attribution and chart controls.

Provider quotes are indicative and may be delayed or unavailable; they are not Invest.to execution quotes. Illustrative charts elsewhere must be identified as illustrations. Never invent quote values or present synthetic performance as live data.

## Voice

Clear. Considered. Human.

Explain before persuading. Name the product and the action. Write for someone making a decision. Describe risk, costs and limitations with the same clarity as features.

Use: “Understand the contract before you trade.”

Avoid: “Guaranteed returns”, “risk-free trading”, “instant profits”, invented client numbers, unverified licences, or unsupported execution and withdrawal promises.

## Service content controls

The owner confirms Invest.to is live. Describe the trading service, MetaTrader 5 and account range in the present tense. Publish account, payment, login and platform links only when their actual destinations are supplied and verified. Eligibility must be assessed country by country, subject to the published region restrictions in the risk disclosure.

Registration is not interchangeable with regulatory authorization. Use the owner-supplied risk disclosure, registration statement and restricted-region list consistently. Do not imply that these disclosures verify regulatory permissions or replace the applicable client agreements.
